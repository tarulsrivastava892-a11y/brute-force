DID YOU KNOW?

Small choices add up.

  • Recycling one ton of paper can save about 17 trees.
  • Food waste in landfills produces methane, a harmful greenhouse gas.
  • Recycling plastic can use up to 80% less energy than making it new.
  • Separating waste can increase recycling rates by up to 50%.
